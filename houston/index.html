Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can save you from a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Houston HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of the year.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
